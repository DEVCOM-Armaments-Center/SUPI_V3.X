## Scope

The small Universal Payload Interface (sUPI) is a **reference design** to create a baseline for a
lethality interface between payloads and platforms. The intent is for the design to evolve as
technologies evolve.

## Submitting Recommendations

Recommendations for improvements should be submitted to **PM CCS**, **PM UAS**, **PM SWAR**, and
**DEVCOM AC** at the current point in time. Refer to your government point of contact to ensure
feedback reaches the appropriate POC.

## Ongoing Work

There are currently science and technology efforts focused on testing the limits of sUPI and the
Common Lethality Integration Kit (CLIK) Design Specification. Considerations for forward and
backward compatibility will be taken into account before revisions are made to the specifications.

sUPI v3.3 shows some of those considerations with the **sUPI medium payload interface card**. The
sUPI medium has been tested with all sUPI v3.X releases.
